Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does escrow payment work?

When you buy a car, your payment goes into EBEVON's secure escrow account — not directly to the seller. You inspect the vehicle, and only when you confirm receipt does the seller get paid. This protects both parties.

How do I become a verified seller?

Sign up, then visit the Seller Verification page. You'll complete a face scan with your device camera and upload a valid government-issued ID (NIN, passport, driver's licence, etc.). Approval takes 24–48 hours.

Can I list my car without verifying?

No. All sellers must complete ID and face verification before their listings go live. This ensures buyer safety and reduces fraud on the platform.

Is delivery available across Nigeria?

Yes. We offer delivery across major Nigerian cities and states. Delivery fees are calculated based on distance. The buyer pays delivery cost, and the car is dispatched only after escrow payment is received.

What happens if the car doesn't match the listing?

Do not confirm receipt. Contact EBEVON support immediately via WhatsApp or phone. Your funds remain in escrow and we will investigate and resolve the dispute. Sellers who misrepresent vehicles face account suspension.

What is the vehicle health score?

The health score is a percentage declared by the seller showing the overall condition of the vehicle. Only Brand New cars have a 100% bar. It covers engine, transmission, exterior, interior, brakes, and tyres.

How does EBEVON make money?

EBEVON charges a 2% transaction fee on completed sales, deducted from the seller's payout after the buyer confirms receipt. There are no fees for buyers or for listing cars.

Can a dealership register on EBEVON?

Yes! When signing up as a seller, dealerships can register their official brand/business name and CAC registration number. A dealership badge will appear on all their listings.

Delivery Information

We deliver to your door anywhere in Nigeria.

Nationwide Coverage

We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT. Lagos, Abuja, PH, Kano, Ibadan and more are covered with priority service.

Escrow-Linked

Car is dispatched only after escrow payment is confirmed. Payment is held until you confirm delivery and inspect the vehicle.

Tracked Delivery

Monitor your delivery in real-time from your buyer dashboard. Get SMS and WhatsApp updates at every stage.

Insured Transit

All vehicles in transit are fully insured. In the rare event of damage during delivery, you are fully protected.

Delivery Timeline

Lagos & Abuja: 1–2 days. South-South & South-East: 2–3 days. North & other regions: 3–5 days.
