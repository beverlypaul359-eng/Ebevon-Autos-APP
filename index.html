How Our Escrow System Works

Your money is safe until you're 100% satisfied with your vehicle.

01

Buyer Pays

Buyer deposits payment into EBEVON's secure escrow account. Funds are held safely.

02

Inspect the Car

Buyer inspects the car physically or receives it via delivery and verifies its condition.

03

Buyer Confirms

Buyer clicks "Confirm Receipt" on the platform — verifying they've seen and accepted the car.

04

Seller Gets Paid

Funds are released from escrow directly to the seller's account. Safe. Fast. Done.
