How EBEVON Works

Whether you're buying or selling a car, EBEVON makes it safe, simple, and transparent.

1. Create a Free Account

Sign up in under 2 minutes. Buyers don't need ID verification — just your name, phone, and email. You're ready to browse immediately.

2. Browse & Filter

Search thousands of verified listings. Filter by brand, year, condition, price range, fuel type, and even minimum health score. Find exactly what fits your budget and lifestyle.

3. Review Photos, Videos & Health Score

Every listing includes 8+ photos from required angles, an exterior walk-around video, interior tour video, and an engine start-up video. The vehicle health bar shows the declared condition at a glance.

Only Brand New cars have a 100% health bar. Always check the score before buying.

4. Pay via Escrow

When you're ready to buy, your payment goes into EBEVON's secure escrow account — not directly to the seller. Your money is completely safe at this stage.

Funds are locked. The seller cannot access them until you confirm.

5. Inspect or Receive Delivery

Visit the seller to inspect the vehicle in person, or request doorstep delivery. Take your time to check that everything matches the listing.

6. Confirm & Complete

Satisfied? Click "Confirm Receipt" in your buyer dashboard. This releases the funds to the seller. The car is yours. If something doesn't match — contact us before confirming. Your money stays locked.

1. Create a Seller Account

Sign up and select "I'm a Seller." Dealerships can also enter their registered business name at this stage for a dealership badge on all listings.

2. Verify Your Identity

Complete our two-step verification: a face scan using your device camera, and upload a valid government ID (NIN, passport, driver's licence, voter's card, etc.). Our team reviews within 24–48 hours.

Verified Seller badge appears on all your listings — buyers trust you more.

3. List Your Car

Fill in car details: brand, model, year, condition, fuel type, mileage, colour, and a full description. Be honest and thorough — detailed listings sell faster.

4. Upload Photos & Videos

Upload at least 8 photos covering all required angles (front, rear, sides, dashboard, engine bay, interior, boot). Then upload 3 videos: exterior walk-around, interior tour, and engine start-up with dashboard view.

Better photos and videos = more buyer confidence = faster sale.

5. Set Vehicle Health Score

Honestly rate your vehicle's overall condition using our health slider. Set individual scores for engine, transmission, exterior, interior, brakes, and tyres. Only Brand New cars get 100%.

Misrepresenting health score is grounds for account suspension and escrow dispute.

6. Get Paid via Escrow

When a buyer purchases your car and confirms receipt after inspection, EBEVON releases the funds directly to your bank account. A 2% platform fee is deducted from the payout. Safe. Fast. Guaranteed.
